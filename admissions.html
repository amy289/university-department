How to Apply

  1. Review the university admission requirements.
  2. Complete the online application form.
  3. Upload the required academic documents.
  4. Submit the application before the deadline.
  5. Follow the university instructions for the next steps.

Documents Checklist

  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • Valid identification document
  • Recent photograph
  • Completed application form
